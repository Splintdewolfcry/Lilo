Got ADHD and taking IELTS/TOEFL soon?


Disclaimer: We're using multiple AI's to provide consistent answers.
I have been teaching English for well over 30 years now. I've had many students who had to take different forms of standardized English tests. Well, we always managed to iron out most commonly used grammar concepts, got my students to relax and focus on the task for the speaking exams but the writing bit... I noticed that we always took longer, partly because some form of "thinking in English" had to be established, taught practical phrases (under the light of these events yada yada). I made sure my students reached their goal, whether it was ranking C1 according to TOEFL conventions or getting over 8. However, it took time and effort, although I help whenever I can, even with generally taught conventions and the different approaches I took, it came down to being an iterative process. It took time, effort *and* iteration over multiple consecutive months. This is what me and my sister are trying to tackle. Making this iterative process less painful.
In our app, we tried to alter this process. I've observed, for some time, that writing on a piece of paper helped out more than writing on Word. So we chose a pen-and-paper approach. If you write legible enough, it can be read. We OCR (optical character recognition) your paper and multiple levels of verification systems run in the background to make sure the answer you get back is actually valuable and in tandem with the objective of these writing tests. Per each answer you get, you also recieve a podcast of the critique that was generated for your essay, multiple tiktok videos (can be turned off!) highlighting different aspects that can be improved and in general consistent reliable improvements.
Gemini and ChatGPT is always a possibility you can try or have tried but we've improved upon them. 
Our app is on Discord. If you pop into our channel and give it a try, it would be most helpful. In our website you can find details of how it works.
Cheers.
